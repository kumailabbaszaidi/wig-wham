Our Origin & Philosophy

Why We Built Wig Wham.

Most e-commerce brands face a costly disconnect: creative agencies design visuals without understanding marketplace flat-files, search indexing, or parent-child variation hierarchies—while recurring administrative and catalog tasks pile up in the background.

Wig Wham was founded to bring both disciplines under one roof. Our marketplace specialists architect high-converting storefronts across Amazon, Walmart, Etsy, and TikTok Shop, while our dedicated Virtual Assistance team runs the daily operational workflows that keep your business organized, responsive, and ready to scale.
