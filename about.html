Why We Built Wig Wham.

Most e-commerce brands face a costly disconnect: creative agencies design visuals without understanding marketplace flat-files or variation hierarchies, while day-to-day administrative and catalog tasks pile up in the background.

Wig Wham was created to bring both sides under one roof. Our marketplace specialists build and optimize listings across Amazon, Walmart, Etsy, and TikTok Shop, while our dedicated Virtual Assistance team handles the recurring operational workflows—from data organization and research to content uploading and routine listing maintenance—that keep a modern brand moving on schedule.
